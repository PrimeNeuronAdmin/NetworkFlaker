Network Flaker

Test your apps on dropping, flaky and slow connections.

Network Flaker is an Android tool for developers and testers. It makes the whole device's connection unreliable on purpose, so you can see how your apps behave on real-world networks. No root is needed, and the connection is restored the moment you stop.

Modes

  • Periodic: down for X seconds, up for Y seconds, on repeat.
  • Random: drop the connection with a chosen probability on every tick.
  • Throttle (Pro): keep the connection working but slow, with a speed cap and added latency.
Private by design. Network Flaker uses Android's VPN service only to create a local tunnel on your device. Your traffic is never sent to a server, logged or collected.
