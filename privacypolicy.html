Network Flaker – Privacy Policy

Developer: PrimeNeuron · Effective date: 28 October 2026

Network Flaker is a testing tool that simulates an unreliable or slow network connection on your Android device. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: it collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device.

Use of the VPN service

Network Flaker uses Android's VpnService only to create a local tunnel on your device. It uses this tunnel to:

  • Periodic and Random modes: discard all traffic during "down" phases, so that apps see a lost connection. No traffic is forwarded or inspected.
  • Throttle mode: forward traffic to its original destination at a reduced speed and with added delay.

The app does not log, store, inspect, modify, sell or send your network traffic to the developer or to any third party. It does not route traffic through any remote server. No personal or sensitive user data is collected through the VPN.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your mode settings and Pro status are stored only on your device.

In-app purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Network Flaker is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
